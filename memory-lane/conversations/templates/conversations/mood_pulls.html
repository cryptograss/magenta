The ramp

Every candidate branch open across our repositories: each branch pushed to githouse for merging, with or without a pull request (GitHub's page for it); the last {{ board.merged|length }} merged. The dot is its temperature: the last push or mention, within a day, three, two weeks, or longer. A 👍 is a person saying "I like this" of the head commit: it merges nothing, and once the branch moves it shows as on an older commit. The plan.
